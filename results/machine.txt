Notes:
- Runs were under WSL2 (Linux VM on Windows), not bare-metal Linux; Windows
  background activity and the hybrid P/E-core scheduler add timing noise,
  which is why every point is a median of 5 (sweep, scaling) or 3 (mlxtend) runs.
- The fpgrowth binary is single-threaded. Runs were sequential, one at a time.
- Every run was capped at a 4 GB address space (prlimit --as) and a 120 s
  timeout (300 s for mlxtend); see scripts/benchlib.py.
- peak_rss_kb is getrusage(ru_maxrss) of the binary itself; it is launched via
  'timeout' so it does not inherit the Python driver's RSS.
